## The project in front of us

Wanderville brings a recognizable campus, farming, home building and decoration, a fishing minigame, companions, shops, character customization and social play into one Godot project. Music and action sounds carry across its title screen, campus, shopping street and fishing activity. The source preserves both this visual/audio experience and the backend work for ownership, inventory, progression, commerce and shared presence.

## Credits

The logo, campus and characters come from the checked-in game assets. The banner was made with the built-in image generator using the actual map capture, buildings, logo, farmer and crops as visual references. Its final export is 1920 × 240 pixels. The full campus image in the README remains an actual Godot scene capture.
